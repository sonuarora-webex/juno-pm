## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's weekly competitive insight brief for Webex Calling
- **Value proposition:** Turn the week’s competitive signals into top three competitive insights I need to act on, each with a source that I can check.  Check that the skill turns competitor announcements into accurate, source-backed product guidance for Webex Calling. The goal is decision quality, not matching one preferred wording.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sources (deal-breaker) | 	Are the material competitor claims traceable to inspected sources that support them, with announcement dates and availability status where relevant? | Yes / No | At least one claim doesn't have a source  | n/a | Each important claim has a source; GA, beta, preview, and planned capabilities are not conflated. |
| Accuracy | 	Does the output avoid fabricated or materially inaccurate facts, including Webex capabilities, roadmap dates, customer demand, competitor features, metrics, and business outcomes? | Yes / No | _ | n/a | No unsupported claim is stated as fact; vendor-reported results are attributed to the vendor. |
| Roadmap sources | Is every Webex capability or roadmap comparison grounded in a current internal product source, or explicitly marked “Not assessed” / “Not found in sources” when evidence is unavailable? | Yes / No | _ | n/a | The output does not infer that absence from searched sources proves absence from the product. It identifies the internal source and date when available. |
| Usefulness (deal-breaker) | Does each insight explain a material customer or business consequence for Webex Calling? | 1 to 5 | Mostly lists features; consequence is unclear. | Explains a plausible impact, with some linkage to customer or business outcomes. | Clearly connects evidence to customer task completion, adoption, revenue/retention, displacement, cost, or strategic position. |
| Does each recommended action respond to a verified gap or defensible strategic opportunity and give a concrete next step? | _ | 1 to 5 | Generic “keep up” or feature-copying recommendation. | Relevant next step, but weakly tied to evidence or a user problem. | Specific PM/product action tied to the user problem, Webex position, and a validation step or measure. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 insights a week, including the ones I felt least sure about
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If our scores differ by 2 or more on any question, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it’s clearer next time.

## Pass bar

All deal breakers = Yes, average score at least 4.0, and no scored question below 3.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Sources: any "no"
- Usefulness: any score of 1
